CURIOUS? GOOD.

Is Momentum Driven just for real estate agents?+

Our initial coaching focus is real estate, drawing on Jeff’s experience. Momentum Driven’s broader purpose is to help business owners build better systems, stronger relationships, and more intentional lives. If you’re in another industry, reach out and tell us what you’re building.

How do I join a coaching session?+

Use “Ask about the next session” to email Candice. She can share the current topics, availability, and pricing. Online booking and payment will be added as the public coaching schedule is finalized.

Will there be resources and a community?+

That’s the vision: coaching, recordings, practical resources, and a place to keep learning and connecting. These are being developed. For now, try the free weekly planner and contact us about coaching.

Can I ask about individual coaching?+

Yes. Tell us about your business, what you’re working toward, and where you feel stuck. We’ll talk through whether there’s a fit and what availability looks like.
